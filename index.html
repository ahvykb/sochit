What happened

Flash floods and landslides displaced tens of thousands

Heavy monsoon rainfall triggered flash floods and a major landslide along the Bhotekoshi and Trishuli river systems on the Nepal–China border in late August 2026. Roads, bridges and homes were destroyed across Rasuwa, Nuwakot and surrounding districts, cutting off entire communities and displacing tens of thousands of people. Recovery is expected to take months, and many displaced families are currently sheltering in schools and temporary camps with limited supplies.

Figures on deaths and missing persons are still being confirmed by Nepal's disaster authorities and are not repeated here in detail, since they continue to change — see the government's NDRRMA updates for the latest official numbers.
